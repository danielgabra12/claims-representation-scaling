## 5. Verdict

- Monthly resolution **does not help and slightly hurts** the bag model (-0.0043 AUROC at the top rung). Handing Arm A the one piece of information the sequence arms hold does not close the gap, so the gap is not explained by temporal resolution.
- Arm A-M still leads the Transformer by **+0.0173 AUROC** at the top rung.

**Caveat that must be stated.** A-M has 11.7x the feature count of Arm A under an identical, untuned XGBoost configuration (same depth, same 800-round cap, same `colsample_bytree=0.8`). Sampling 80% of 9,672 columns is a very different regularisation regime from 80% of 828. So the correct claim is that monthly resolution does not help **under matched, untuned hyperparameters**, not that it cannot help. A retuned A-M is the obvious follow-up and was deliberately not run, because retuning only the arm we want to help would break the parity rule.
