## Reading it

- **No binning beats Arm A on AUROC at any rung.** The best of them at the top rung is Arm A-2 (two 6-month bins) at 0.8261 against Arm A's 0.8279.
- Performance degrades monotonically with binning at the two largest rungs, so the penalty is a smooth dose-response rather than a cliff at the finest binning. Crucially it is already present at **A-2, which has only twice Arm A's column count**, which is the direct answer to the objection that A-M's deficit is an artefact of an 11.7x increase in features.
- **The two effects remain confounded by construction**: finer binning necessarily means more columns, so this sweep shows the penalty grows with binning granularity without isolating whether the mechanism is temporal dilution or feature-count regularisation. What it does establish is the claim the paper needs: giving the order-free bag temporal resolution never helps, at any granularity from two bins to twelve.
- **On AUPRC the picture is weaker and must be reported as such.** At the top rung bin2 is +0.0002 [-0.0024, +0.0028], not distinguishable from zero, bin4 is +0.0008 [-0.0020, +0.0036], not distinguishable from zero. Coarse binning is therefore roughly neutral on the precision-recall metric even where it costs AUROC, and we do not claim it harms both.
