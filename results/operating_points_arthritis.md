The lift quoted here is **enrichment at an alert budget**, which is not the same quantity as the AUPRC-to-base-rate ratio quoted in section 4.1. The latter summarises the whole precision-recall curve; the former is what a fixed-capacity review programme would actually experience. Both are reported because they answer different questions.
