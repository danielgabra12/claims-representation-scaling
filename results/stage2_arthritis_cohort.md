# Stage 2 — Cohort & phenotype: Arthritis (any subtype)

_Generated by `src.scale.data.cohort`. dx-only arm of the confound-hardened phenotype; the treatment arm is PENDING sign-off and will be added additively._

## Design
- **Diagnosis codes:** `^(M0[5-8]|M1[0235-9]|M45|L405)` on `CODE_TYPE='DX'` (ICD-10, no dots).
- **Index date:** `FIRST_MONTH + 12` (13th enrolled month).
- **Baseline (features):** 12 months before index. **Prediction window:** 12 months from index.
- **Eligibility:** age 18-89 at index; continuous 24-month enrollment (12+12); arthritis-free at index (not prevalent).
- **Label:** `1` if >=2 distinct qualifying-dx months within the prediction window, else `0`.
- **Temporal holdout:** train index-years [2020, 2021, 2022], test index-year 2023.

## Results
- **Cohort size:** 595,758 patients. **Positives:** 11,750 (**1.97%** onset within 12 months).
- **Age at index:** min 18, mean 44.6, max 83.
- **Sex:** F=317,155, M=278,559, U=44
